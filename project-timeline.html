Project Timeline

A Granular HCI Design Process

Week 2: Topic Confirmation
📜

Concept Definition

Confirmed the topic as "EduMap Journey," aiming to transform the anxiety-inducing postgraduate application process into an engaging, RPG-style interactive learning pathway.

Week 3: Mixed-Methods Research
🗣️

Qualitative Interviews

Interviewed potential study abroad students and academic advisors to uncover deeper psychological pain points regarding school comparison, deadline anxiety, and motivation drop-offs.

📊

Quantitative Questionnaire

Distributed a targeted survey (total n=44) to statistically validate issues like information overload, and to measure user acceptance of game-based metaphorical guidance.

Week 4: Synthesis & Empathy Mapping
🎭

Persona Development

Created dual-stakeholder Personas representing both the overwhelmed student needing staged guidance and the academic advisor requiring centralized tracking.

🧭

User Journey Mapping

Mapped the end-to-end emotional and practical student journey, pinpointing the critical transition from early-stage "Goal Confusion" to late-stage "Execution Stress."

📚

Literature & Product Research

Analyzed 4 academic papers and 4 commercial products to identify "The Gap," structuring findings into a strict 3 Pros & 3 Cons matrix to justify our gamified approach.

Week 5: Ideation & Prototyping
🎯

Requirement Analysis

Translated research insights into a Core Alignment matrix (Requirement ➔ Design Goal ➔ Feature) and explicitly defined the 3 "Playful Must-Haves" for system engagement.

🪄

Ideation & Low-fi Design

Conducted "Crazy 8s" rapid sketching to explore UI metaphors, followed by creating clickable Low-Fi wireframes in Figma for the "Star-map" navigation and "Alchemy Crafting" stages.

Week 5–8: System Development
🏗️

High-Fi Demo Implementation

Built the decoupled full-stack web demo (Vue 3 + Spring Boot) featuring stage-based gameplay, interactive progress tracking, and bilingual content delivery.

⚡

AI Vibe Coding Process

Leveraged AI-assisted development (Cursor/ChatGPT) to accelerate frontend layout iterations, CSS styling, and data-state debugging, combined with strict manual code reviews.

Week 8: User Evaluation
🛡️

Alpha Usability Testing

Conducted formal usability testing on the deployed demo with 3 real target users. Collected both qualitative confusion points and quantitative UEQ metrics (Attractiveness, Pragmatic, and Hedonic qualities).

Week 9–10: Refinement & Delivery
🛠️

Iterative Refinement

Executed concrete UI/UX shifts based on the feedback log: upgraded vague system tags to concrete academic metrics (Shift 1) and replaced weak notifications with actionable milestone modals (Shift 2).

🏆

Final Demo & Deployment

Finalized the live deployment on Alibaba Cloud, compiled the comprehensive HCI Process Portfolio, and produced the demo video showcasing the complete user-centric evidence chain.
